# Volunteering that moves your career forward

The radar reads Volunteer Connector (which powers GoVolunteer.ca) and Indeed once a day, and scores
each role on **career value**: how much the work overlaps with entry-level analyst, banking and
investment jobs, whether it produces a concrete resume line, the organization's credibility, and a
realistic time commitment. Good ones go to the **Volunteer** tab. Roles scoring 75+ are emailed as
**"Volunteer match"**.

**What volunteering can and can't do:** relevant volunteer work counts as experience with many
employers, especially nonprofits, government and credit unions. It's strongest where postings say
"experience *in* X" or "1–2 years preferred". It rarely satisfies "2+ years of *professional*
experience *required*" at large banks, but it gives you specific stories, tools and references to
use in interviews.

## Programs worth joining directly

These recruit year-round or by season instead of posting individual openings. Check each site for
current details before you commit.

| Program | Why it fits you | Builds toward |
|---|---|---|
| **CRA Community Volunteer Income Tax Program (CVITP)**: free tax clinics run by United Way, neighbourhood houses and settlement agencies | Volunteers usually sign up in the fall and are trained before tax season (Feb–Apr). Client-facing financial work with real documents, at volume. | Banking (CL), KYC and onboarding, financial services rep |
| **Junior Achievement BC**: financial-literacy volunteer | You teach budgeting, credit and investing to students. Builds on your tutoring and adds a recognized finance brand to your resume. | Banking (CL), advisor assistant |
| **Credit Counselling Society** (BC-based): volunteer roles | Money conversations with real clients in hardship; nonprofit credit and budgeting work. | Banking (CL), credit analyst |
| **Statistics Without Borders**: remote pro-bono statistics projects | Real data projects for NGOs, reviewed by experienced statisticians. Strong analyst portfolio evidence. | Data and research analyst (AN) |
| **DataKind** (DataCorps and events) and **Data for Good** (Vancouver chapter) | Team data projects for social-sector clients. Networking with working analysts. | Data, BI and research analyst (AN) |
| **Catchafire**: skills-based remote projects (financial models, dashboards, survey analysis) | Defined projects with a deliverable and a nonprofit reference. Filter for finance and data. | AN and INV |
| **Nonprofit board or treasurer roles**: Vantage Point (Vancouver) training, and roles the radar finds on Volunteer Connector | Budgets, financial statements and governance. Treasurer roles at small, credible nonprofits read well for investment and finance work. | INV, financial analyst |
| **UBC Vancouver School of Economics**: research-assistant volunteering | Email two or three professors whose work matches your projects (housing, development, public finance) and offer a few hours a week of data work. Can turn into paid RA work and a strong reference. | Research and economic analyst (AN) |

## Getting the most from it

- **One substantial role beats three light ones.** Aim for a role where, after 3 months, you can write a
  bullet like "analyzed [X] survey responses in Excel and presented findings to the board".
- **Ask for a defined deliverable and a reference up front.** Most coordinators are glad to agree.
- **Add it to your master resume and evidence bank as soon as you start.** The radar and your Claude
  Project will then count it when screening and tailoring.
